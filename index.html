About MTG Unframed

The artists have always been one of my favorite parts of Magic: The Gathering. This project started from a simple wish: to see their work together, not as isolated cards, but as bodies of work that span sets, years, and changing visual directions.

Look beyond the card

Thousands of artists have shaped the visual identity of Magic. Their work tells the story of the game’s history, but it can easily disappear behind the frame, the text box, and the mana cost.

Beautiful Art, Forgotten Cards

In play, a card is judged by what it does: is it strong, is it playable, is it still legal? Here, none of that matters. MTG Unframed gives you room to look at the art for its own sake, without thinking about mechanics or whether the card is any good. Some of Magic’s most beautiful pieces belong to cards that rarely see play or have long been forgotten. When a card stops being relevant, its art shouldn’t be forgotten with it.

Art Across the Years

MTG Unframed brings those artists forward. Follow one artist across different worlds and eras, or move between sets and watch the style of the game change over time.

This is a gallery, not a card database. You won’t find rules text, prices, or deck tools here, just the art and the people who made it. Stay a while and enjoy it.
